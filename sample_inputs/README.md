## 📋 Ready-to-Use Test Prompts

### Scenario 1: Current Room + Budget ($12k)
- **Attach**: `outdated_kitchen.jpg`
- **Prompt**:
  > *"Here is a photo of my current kitchen (honey oak cabinets, laminate counters). What can I improve here with a $12,000 budget in a modern farmhouse style? Please preserve the layout and provide a complete cost and timeline roadmap."*

### Scenario 2: Current Room + Inspiration
- **Attach**: `outdated_kitchen.jpg` (Photo 1) and `kitchen_inspiration.jpg` (Photo 2)
- **Prompt**:
  > *"Here are two photos: Photo 1 is my existing kitchen, and Photo 2 is my target inspiration. Transform my current kitchen to achieve the inspiration aesthetic (white shaker cabinets, quartz counters, black hardware) while strictly keeping the exact appliance and cabinet layout. What is the estimated cost and roadmap?"*

### Scenario 3: Bathroom Spa Transformation ($20k)
- **Attach**: `outdated_bathroom.jpg` (Photo 1) and `bathroom_inspiration.jpg` (Photo 2)
- **Prompt**:
  > *"Here is my dated 1980s master bathroom (Photo 1) and my inspiration photo (Photo 2). I want a clean, modern spa retreat with walk-in glass shower, fluted vanity, and warm lighting with a $20k budget. Keep the plumbing fixtures in their current locations."*

### Scenario 4: Text-Only Renovation Request
- **Prompt**:
  > *"I want to renovate my 10x14 living room. Currently has worn brown carpeting, dark wood paneling, and poor lighting. Want a contemporary warm minimalist aesthetic with wide-plank white oak flooring, limewash neutral walls, recessed LED lighting, and a modern fireplace mantel. Budget is $15,000."*

### Scenario 5: Iterative Refinement
- **Prompt** (after rendering is generated):
  > *"I love the rendering, but could you make the kitchen cabinets a soft warm cream (Benjamin Moore Swiss Coffee) instead of bright white, and change the hardware to brushed brass?"*
